# Source attribution

The solver methods, interpolation coefficients, and example equations
are adapted from [BVP_SOLVER and BVP_SOLVER-2](https://cs.smu.ca/~muir/BVP_SOLVER_Webpage.shtml). They are used to solve ordinary differential equation boundary value problems.

- BVP_SOLVER-2: Copyright (c) 2012, Jason Boisvert, Paul Muir, Ray Spiteri.
- Original BVP_SOLVER: Copyright (c) 2006, Paul Muir and Larry Shampine.
- Original paper: L. F. Shampine, P. H. Muir, H. Xu, _A user-friendly Fortran BVP solver_, J. Numer. Anal. Indust. Appl. Math., 1, 2006, 201–217.
